## 1. Introduction
**ARGUS-Thesis** is not just another grammar checker. It is an **Adversarial Governance Engine**. 
Think of it as a "Digital Reviewer #2" that doesn't sleep. It subjects your academic manuscript to a rigorous, multi-agent stress test to find logical contradictions, statistical errors, and missing novelty *before* you submit to a journal.

*   **Our Promise**: "Privacy by Physics". Your data is processed in ephemeral RAM and never stored in a database.
*   **The Goal**: Increasing your acceptance rate by prevalidating your core arguments.

## 2. Quick Start
1.  **Sign Up**: Create an account at `app.argus-thesis.com`.
2.  **Top Up**: Purchase a set of Audit Credits (or join your University's Organization).
3.  **Upload**: Click "New Audit" and drag your PDF manuscript (or paste text).
4.  **Extract**: Click "Extract Claims" to let the Thesis Constructor parse your paper.
5.  **Audit**: Select critical claims and run agents (e.g., "Thesis Destroyer").
6.  **Report**: Download your "Governance Certificate" PDF.

## 3. The Interface Glossary
When you enter the **Dashboard**, you will see three main panels:

### 3.1 The Manuscript Viewer (Left)
*   **Purpose**: Displays your uploaded PDF or text.
*   **Highlights**: As agents find issues, they will highlight relevant text in Red (Contradiction) or Green (Support).

### 3.2 The Claims Matrix (Center)
*   **The "AST"**: This list represents the "Abstract Syntax Tree" of your paper—the atomic arguments you are making.
*   **Status Icons**:
    *   ⚪ **Pending**: Not yet audited.
    *   🟢 **Verified**: Passed rigorous checks.
    *   🔴 **Falsified**: An agent successfully broke this claim.
    *   🟡 **Unverified**: Novel claim with no external evidence found (this is often good!).

### 3.3 The Agent Console (Right)
*   **Swarm Controller**: This is where you call specific agents.
*   **Logs**: Real-time "thought chains" of the AI agents as they critique your work.

## 4. The Audit Workflow
Be strategic. You don't need to run every agent on every sentence.

### Phase 1: The Structure Check
*   **Agent**: `Thesis Constructor`
*   **Action**: Automatically runs on upload.
*   **Goal**: Ensure your paper *has* a thesis. If this fails, rewrite your Abstract.

### Phase 2: The Attack (Logic)
*   **Agent**: `Thesis Destroyer`
*   **Best For**: Your central hypothesis.
*   **What it does**: It tries to find logical fallacies (circular reasoning, strawman arguments).
*   **When to run**: On your main "Results" claims.

### Phase 3: The Methodology Check (Stats)
*   **Agent**: `Methodology Analyst`
*   **Best For**: "Method" sections and "Results".
*   **What it does**: Scans for "p-hacking", sample size issues, and mismatched metrics.

### Phase 4: The Novelty Check
*   **Agent**: `Literature Reviewer` (powered by Perplexity)
*   **Best For**: Introduction and Related Work.
*   **What it does**: Searches live academic databases to see if your "novel" idea was actually done in 2018.

## 5. Interpreting Your Report
Upon completion, you can download a **PDF Governance Report**.

### The "Verdict"
*   **PUBLISHABLE (Score > 85)**: Your paper is logically sound. Minor revisions may be needed.
*   **REVISE_MAJOR (Score 60-84)**: Significant gaps in logic or support.
*   **REJECT (Score < 60)**: Fundamental flaws detected. Do not submit yet.

### Common Failure Modes
1.  **"Hollow Citations"**: Citing a paper that doesn't actually support your claim.
2.  **"Circular Definition"**: Defining a metric in terms of itself.
3.  **"Baseline Omission"**: Comparing your model to a weak baseline instead of SOTA.

## 6. Enterprise & Organization Management
For Lab Directors and Department Heads.

### Creating an Organization
1.  Go to **Settings > Organization**.
2.  Click **"Create Organization"**.
3.  Name your Lab (e.g., "MIT Media Lab").
4.  You are now the **Admin**.

### Managing Members
*   **Invite**: Add students by email. They will receive an invite code.
*   **Roles**: Currently, all invited members have equal access to the credit pool.
*   **Quotas**: You can view usage logs to see who is using the most credits.

## 7. Billing & Credits
*   **Personal**: Buy credits directly ($14.99/pack).
*   **Enterprise**: The Admin buys credits. All members "draw down" from this central pool automatically.
*   **Refunds**: If the system crashes or hangs during an audit, a refund is automatic. If the system simply "disagrees" with your paper, that is `working as intended` and is not refundable.
