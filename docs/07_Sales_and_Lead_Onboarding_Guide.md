**Target Audience:** Sales Teams, Customer Success, Account Executives
**Last Updated:** March 2026

---

## 1. Product Elevator Pitch

**What is ARGUS?** 
ARGUS is an interactive **Adversarial AI Audit Platform** for academic research and claims. It ingests research papers and deploys a "Swarm" of specialized AI agents to rigorously interrogate the methodology, logically attack the thesis, and audit the mathematical formalisms, eliminating human oversight and scaling rigorous peer review.

**Who is this for?**
- **Universities and Academic Submissions:** Researchers seeking aggressive adversarial feedback before submitting their work to high-tier journals.
- **Enterprise R&D:** Auditing internal engineering schemas and claims.

## 3. The "Aha" Moment to Demo

When pitching to researchers, you must show them the specific value of hallucination-free, high-context AI review.

1. **Step 1:** Upload a paper that has known methodological weaknesses (e.g. a small sample size claiming broad generalizations).
2. **Step 2:** Watch the UI visually spawn the different Adversary Agents. This shows the customer they are paying for *computational depth*, not a simple ChatGPT wrapper.
3. **Step 3:** Highlight the `METHODOLOGY_PROSECUTOR` output accurately quoting text from the paper demanding tighter boundary conditions.

## 4. Key Objections & Rebuttals

*   **"ChatGPT analyzes papers for me for $20/mo already."**
    *   *Rebuttal:* ChatGPT is a single generalist model built to please the user horizontally. ARGUS runs 6 simultaneous agents executing highly-adversarial frameworks explicitly designed to destroy theoretical arguments and audit academic structure reliably.
*   **"Is my IP safe?"**
    *   *Rebuttal:* Yes. ARGUS operates on a Zero-Retention Architecture. As soon as the memory buffer dumps its evaluation to the end-client, the paper and prompt embeddings are permanently garbage-collected.
*   **"But AI hallucinates omissions often!"**
    *   *Rebuttal:* As of March 2026, ARGUS forces *Anti-Hallucination Execution* on validators. The AI is strictly mandated to scan the entire, un-truncated context pipeline and physically quote text strings when bringing methodological flaw accusations against the author.
